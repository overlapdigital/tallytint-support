Frequently asked questions

How do I add a countdown?

Tap +, choose Days until or Days since, and enter a name and date. Choose a colour and a format, then tap Save.

How do I correct a check-in?

Tap the event card, then find Check-in history. Choose Week or Month and tap the date you want to change. Tap Save to keep the change.

How do I choose what my widget shows?

Add a small or medium Tallytint widget to your Home Screen. Touch and hold it, choose Edit Widget, then pick the event you want to see. If it says Open Tallytint to add an event, create an event in the app first.

How do I delete an event and its history?

Open the event card and tap Delete event. Confirm to remove the event and its check-ins. See the privacy policy for more about local storage and backups.

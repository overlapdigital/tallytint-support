Tallytint Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

1. About Tallytint

Tallytint is an iPhone app for countdowns, day counts and habit tracking. It is developed by Thomas Williams in the United Kingdom. Thomas Williams is the controller of personal information you send with a support or privacy enquiry.

2. Data in the app

Tallytint saves event names, dates, colours, display settings and check-in dates on your iPhone. The app and its Home Screen widgets use these records to show your countdowns, streaks and habit totals.

Tallytint does not require an account or send your event and check-in data to the developer. The app has no advertising, analytics, tracking or third-party crash-reporting services. Tallytint does not provide cloud synchronisation.

3. Retention and deletion

Your records stay on your iPhone until you edit or delete them, or delete the app and its local data. To remove a record and its check-ins, open its card and choose Delete event. You can edit individual check-ins in Check-in history.

Apple’s device backups may include app data, depending on your settings. Manage any backup copies separately through Apple’s backup settings. The developer cannot access or delete the app data on your iPhone.

4. Support and privacy enquiries

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send, including any attachments. We use it to answer your enquiry, provide support and handle follow-up. Sharing additional details is voluntary. Please send only what is relevant to your enquiry.

Under the UK GDPR, we rely on our legitimate interest in responding to users and supporting Tallytint for ordinary enquiries. We rely on legal obligation where we are required to respond to a data protection request.

We use Gmail to receive, store and reply to enquiries. Google describes its handling of information and the safeguards for processing it outside the UK in its Privacy Policy and data transfer information.

We retain correspondence while an enquiry is open and for as long as needed for follow-up, any dispute or a legal obligation. We delete correspondence when it is no longer needed.

5. Visiting this website

GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security and handles technical request data in accordance with the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not add analytics, advertising or tracking cookies to this site.

6. Your privacy rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict the use of or receive a copy of personal information we hold about you.

You can object to our use of your personal information where we rely on legitimate interests.

To exercise your rights or raise a concern, use the contact address below. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.

7. Contact and policy updates

For a privacy enquiry or request, email Thomas Williams at meteorapp.support@gmail.com with Tallytint privacy in the subject.

When our practices change, we will update this page and the date above.
